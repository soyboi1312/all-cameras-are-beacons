6. METHOD AND LIMITS

All 497,982 lines were inspected programmatically. BLE manufacturer IDs and
16-bit services were read by walking length/type/data elements, not searching
for a hex substring inside unrelated payloads. 59,304 BLE and 277,073 Wi-Fi
records parsed into complete expected log fields. Among parsed BLE records,
35 have invalid/truncated hex or incomplete AD elements; damaged serial output
also leaves some records outside the expected format. These are recorded
observations, not a complete census of every radio packet the board received.

All principal evidence lines quoted above are intact. Wi-Fi full frames and
flight coordinates are unavailable in this text format. Confidence numbers
are firmware evidence grades, not measured probabilities. No firmware,
signature rules, or false-positive classifications were changed for this review.

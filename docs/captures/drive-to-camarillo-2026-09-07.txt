  b. The windows are contiguous and non-overlapping. Each closes ~14m50s in, seconds before the
     next opens. The chain runs unbroken from the first boundary after capture start to capture end.
  c. Within a window the payload holds once its format settles: nine of the ten addresses carry

This is one Apple device rotating its RPA and ephemeral key on a 15-minute timer. Note the first
window opens in the NEARBY format (07FF4C0012020002, type 0x12 length 0x02) and the rest are in the
offline format, i.e. it lost its owner's network during the run.

Consequences:
  - The external population is 21 detections across 15 addresses, not 31 across 25.
  - Every one of the 10 is strong-signal (-39..-54). The 15 external addresses are almost all weak
    (-79..-91), consistent with passing traffic. Signal strength separates the two populations
    cleanly here.
  - This is a self/companion detection reaching the classifier at conf=85. Worth a filter, and
    worth remembering before any future count of "trackers seen on a drive".
